The drive: Greensboro → Oldsmar

~650 miles · roughly 10.5–11 hours of driving
I-85 S out of Greensboro, picking up I-95 S through South Carolina and Georgia, then across to Tampa Bay via I-4/I-275. Leaving at 8 AM with a lunch/gas stop around the SC/GA line (Savannah is a natural halfway break) puts arrival in Oldsmar around 6:30–7:30 PM, depending on stops and traffic. Long day — build in a real stretch break every 2–3 hours.
